Class VoiceLineMelodyVoice

Namespace
SubVoxPro
Assembly
SubVoxPro.dll

Plays a short blip on every spoken word of its emitter, "Animal Crossing" style, in every language and with nothing to generate: each blip follows the intonation of the word in the audio playing (source or dub), and gets a light random pitch when the line has no audio or no clear voice. Put it next to a VoiceLineEmitter and pick a register per character.

public class VoiceLineMelodyVoice : MonoBehaviour
Inheritance
object
VoiceLineMelodyVoice

Examples

VoiceLineMelodyVoice voice = _villager.GetComponent<VoiceLineMelodyVoice>();
voice.Mode = EVoiceLineMelodyMode.Replace;
voice.BasePitch = 5f;
_villager.GetComponent<VoiceLineEmitter>().Play(greeting);
