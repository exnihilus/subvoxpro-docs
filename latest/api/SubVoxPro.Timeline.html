Namespace SubVoxPro.Timeline

Classes

VoiceLineAudioPlayableAsset

Audio clip paired with a VoiceLineClip on a native Audio Track. It shows and previews the source audio in the editor, and plays the dub of CurrentLanguage when one exists. The language is read when the Timeline graph is built (when the director starts playing), so a dub never switches in the middle of a cutscene.

VoiceLineClip

Timeline clip backed by a VoiceLineData asset. Dropping a VoiceLineData onto a VoiceLineTrack creates one of these automatically, and its length defaults to the longest audio of the voice line (source or dub). Dropping a VoiceLineSequenceData instead is captured by the transient VoiceLineSequenceData field and expanded into one clip per step by the clip editor (which clears the field back to null).

VoiceLineEventMarker

A voice line event baked as a Timeline marker, fired through a VoiceLineTimelineEventReceiver.

VoiceLineEventTrack

Timeline track holding the markers baked from voice line events.

VoiceLineTimelineEventReceiver

Raises baked voice line events on SubVox when their marker is reached. Added to the PlayableDirector by the bake action.

VoiceLineTrack

Timeline track that drives voice line subtitles, synced to the director clock. Audio is played by a paired native Audio Track (created automatically on drop), so this track only publishes subtitles on the SubVox for any display to pick up.
