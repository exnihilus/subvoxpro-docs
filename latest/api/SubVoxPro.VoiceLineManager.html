Class VoiceLineManager

Namespace
SubVoxPro
Assembly
SubVoxPro.dll

The scene component behind SubVox: holds the 2D VoiceLineEmitter that Play(VoiceLineData, EVoiceLinePriority, EVoiceLinePlayMode) uses (narration, dialogue), pauses or stops every emitter at once, ducks the music while a voice plays, and publishes the subtitle of the most important line playing — highest priority first, then the most recently started.
