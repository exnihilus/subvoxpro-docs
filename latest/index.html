Where to start

SubVox
The entry point for code: play lines, pause or stop them all, set the language and the subtitle options, and listen to what plays.
VoiceLineEmitter
Makes a character speak, in 3D, one line at a time, with priorities and a queue.
VoiceLinePlayback
What every play call returns: follow a line, stop it, or wait for it in a coroutine.
VoiceLineData
A voice line asset, with its subtitles, translations, dubs, speakers and events.
