Class VoiceLineEmitter

Namespace
SubVoxPro
Assembly
SubVoxPro.dll

Plays voice lines through its own AudioSource, one at a time, with their events and synced subtitles. Put one on each character that speaks and set its AudioSource to 3D to hear the voice from there: several emitters play at the same time, and the VoiceLineManager shows the subtitle of the most important line playing.

PlayExternal(VoiceLineData, IVoiceLineAudioClock, EVoiceLinePriority, EVoiceLinePlayMode)

Plays a voice line whose audio you play yourself (FMOD, Wwise, a video…): its subtitles and events follow audio_clock instead of this emitter's AudioSource, and it completes when the clock stops playing. Once it starts, start the audio of CurrentAudioLanguage. Same rules as Play(VoiceLineData, EVoiceLinePriority, EVoiceLinePlayMode); Pause(), Seek(float) and Stop() only affect the subtitles and events, so control your audio too.

public VoiceLinePlayback PlayExternal(VoiceLineData voice_line, IVoiceLineAudioClock audio_clock, EVoiceLinePriority priority = EVoiceLinePriority.Normal, EVoiceLinePlayMode mode = EVoiceLinePlayMode.Replace)

Moves the current voice line to a time, in seconds. Events between the two times do not fire. Has no effect on a line played with PlayExternal(VoiceLineData, IVoiceLineAudioClock, EVoiceLinePriority, EVoiceLinePlayMode): seek your audio instead, the line follows it.
